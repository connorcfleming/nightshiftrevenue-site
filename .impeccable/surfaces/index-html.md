# Surface brief: index.html (landing page)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: owners of established small businesses (any industry) with a list of past inquiries that never booked. Action: request the free lead audit (Netlify form `demo`, hero quick form + full form). Proof available: the mechanism (illustrative street, Dana's thread), consent rules, plain pay-per-booking model, founder block (placeholders until the owner supplies facts). Constraints: no invented results, prices or testimonials; one quiet illustrative label; owner-pinned palette midnight blue + green + moonlight yellow; no robots, no neon glow.

Unresolved: founder name/photo/city/story; contact email; whether a price ever appears.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Your old leads are a street of dark houses. Overnight, while the office is empty, the crew's conversations light a few windows green, and a porch light turns moonlight yellow for each booking. Refuses the dark-neon AI template and the split hero with a phone mockup.

OWN-WORLD: Midnight-blue sky and flat silhouette houses drawn as crisp geometry; window light is flat green, never glowing; moonlight yellow is reserved for a booked appointment and the moon. Light display face with mid-century house-number elegance (Josefin Sans) over Schibsted Grotesk body; times stay in proportional figures (Schibsted's tabular set spaces the colon). Kerb labels under each house are the lead log. Calm, generous dark space; no cards-in-a-row.

STORY: The visitor sees their own dead list as a sleeping street, watches one lead come back in a real thread, learns how a night runs and that they pay only per booked appointment, reads the consent rules and who runs it, and ends at dawn with a booked calendar and the audit form.
