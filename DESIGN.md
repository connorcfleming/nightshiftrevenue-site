# Night Shift Revenue brand system

The owner-supplied Night Shift Revenue Brand Board.png governs the brand. Charcoal surfaces, electric lime (#C6FF00), off-white (#F5F5E9), olive (#3A442E), and muted gray (#9CA3AF) replace the former blue palette. The existing street-of-leads narrative, page content and lead-audit flow are retained.

The crescent, ascending bars and upward arrow are recreated as transparent SVG geometry. The two-line horizontal logo embeds self-hosted Oxanium, a close squared-letter interpretation of the supplied lettering; the exact original font was not supplied. Headings use Oxanium 700–800, body copy uses Schibsted Grotesk.

Tagline: Turn missed opportunities into revenue.

Electric lime identifies actions and live conversations. Off-white identifies bookings, with explicit status labels. Focus, reduced-motion support and form states retain their existing behavior. The same identity applies to header, footer, policy pages, favicon and Apple touch icon. Header logo widths: 248px desktop, 212px tablet, 174px mobile, 147px below 360px.

Sources: assets/brand/icon.svg and lockup-horizontal-dark.svg. Raster icons are rendered from SVG. Pre-update files are preserved in .brand-backup-20261012/.

Owner color update: main page and hero background #111210, accent #C6FF00. Supporting surfaces use charcoal and olive tones from the original brand board.

Body text update: all reading text, secondary descriptions, captions, form labels and placeholders use white (#FFFFFF). Accent and status colors retain their semantic roles; text on light buttons stays dark for contrast.
