    1. Day 1, evening

      Your list goes out in small batches

      We start with a test of your freshest 2,000 leads, sent during reasonable hours in each lead's time zone.

    2. Two short lines

      "Is this the same Chris?"

      A casual, friendly text with their first name and the service they asked about. It reads like a person checking in, not like an ad.

    3. Whenever they reply

      Questions about price, timing and trust get handled. No reply? Up to three friendly bumps over the following days.

    4. Next morning

      Appointments land on your calendar. Your team walks in knowing what the lead wants and when.
