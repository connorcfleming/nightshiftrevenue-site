How it works

Four stations, in order. Nothing moves to the next one until the last one is done.

  1. 1

    Free reactivation audit

    Send us an export of your CRM. We look at lead age, source, volume and how each contact opted in, then tell you straight whether there's enough here to work.

  2. 2

    Messages in your voice

    We write the conversation for your business: your offer, your qualifying questions, your booking rules. Nothing goes out until you approve it.

  3. 3

    One conversation at a time

    Texts go out in measured batches. Our AI holds each conversation, answers questions and asks yours, and we step in when something needs a person. STOP ends messages immediately.

  4. 4

    Appointments on your calendar

    Qualified leads are booked straight onto your team's calendar with the conversation attached, so your rep walks in knowing the story.

You pay when it works.

No retainer. Terms are set per partner after the free audit, in writing, before a single text goes out.

Pay per booked appointment

Before launch we agree, in writing, what counts as a booked appointment for your business. You pay for each one. If the campaign books none, you owe nothing.

Revenue share

For the right partner: instead of a per-appointment fee, we take an agreed share of closed sales that come from reactivated leads. It suits businesses with long sales cycles and high-value jobs.

Who it's for

Established businesses with a real sales process and a database full of people who once raised their hand.

Good fit

  • You sell something worth thousands, not tens: solar, roofing, remodeling, insurance, legal, financial or B2B services.
  • You have a sales team, or at least someone who runs appointments.
  • Your CRM holds past inquiries, quotes or consults that never closed.
  • You can show how those contacts agreed to hear from you.

Not a fit

  • Purchased, scraped or rented lists.
  • Contacts who never gave your business permission to text them.
  • Low-price products that sell without a conversation.
  • You need brand-new leads. We work the ones you already have.

How we text your leads

Your customers' trust is the asset. These rules hold on every campaign.

  • Permission first. We only text contacts who gave your business permission to contact them by text. We review consent and opt-out records with you before launch, and anyone we can't verify stays off the list.
  • Every first message says who's texting. It names your business and explains how to stop: reply STOP.
  • Opt-outs are final. STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END or QUIT ends messages right away. We record the opt-out and give it back to you so your team doesn't text that person again.
  • Reasonable hours. Outreach goes out during reasonable hours in the lead's own time zone. Replies are answered when the lead writes in.
  • Your do-not-contact list wins. Anyone on it is removed before launch.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Texting rules vary by state and change over time; the messaging rules for your campaign are agreed with you before launch. This page is not legal advice.

Questions owners ask

Is this a chatbot?

Not the kind on a website. It's a texting campaign run on your old leads, with AI writing replies inside rules we set with you: what to say, what to ask, when to book and when to hand off. We watch the conversations and step in when something needs a person.

These leads have been called to death. Why would they answer now?

Timing changes. Someone who wasn't ready last year may be ready now, and a short text is easier to answer than a sales call. The free audit tells you whether your list is worth working before you commit to anything.

Will it sound like us?

It should. We write the messages from how your team already talks to customers, and you approve them before launch.

Which CRM do you work with?

We start from an export of your contacts (a CSV file), so most CRMs work. Appointments go onto your team's calendar.

What counts as a booked appointment?

We define it with you in writing before launch, for example a qualified lead confirmed on your calendar for a specific time. You only pay for appointments that meet that definition.
