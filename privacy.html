Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy explains how Night Shift Revenue ("Night Shift Revenue," "we," "us") collects and uses information on nightshiftrevenue.com and when we run text-message campaigns for business clients.

Information we collect on this website

  • What you give us: when you fill out our form, we collect your name, email, phone number and whether you agreed to receive texts.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider may log your IP address, browser type, pages visited and the time of the visit for security and operations.
  • Cookies and tracking: We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. Our form provider may set cookies needed for the form to work.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request and schedule a call.
  • To text or call you about your request, only if you checked the consent box.
  • To operate, secure and improve this website.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Text messaging

If you agree to receive text messages from us, message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or text-messaging opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. Text-message opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party except the service providers that deliver our messages.

Data we handle for business clients

When a business hires us to contact its own leads, we process those leads' contact details and message history on the business's behalf and under its instructions. We use that data only to run that business's campaign, we honor opt-outs immediately, and we return or delete client data when the engagement ends, as agreed in our client contract. Questions about a business's own data practices should go to that business.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run our business, such as website hosting, form handling, our CRM and our text-messaging provider (Netlify and HighLevel), and when the law requires it. We do not sell personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while we have a business relationship with you and as long as the law requires. We keep opt-out records so we can keep honoring them.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop messages.
  • Email or call us to access, correct or delete the information you gave us.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws.

Children

This website is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.
